# Internal Memo — Northgate Logistics

**To:** All Warehouse Staff
**From:** Dana Whitfield, Operations Manager

**Re:** Holiday closure, new scanner rollout, and parking changes

## Holiday Closure
The warehouse will be **closed Friday, May 22 through Monday, May 25** for the Memorial Day weekend. Normal operations resume Tuesday, May 26 at 6:00 AM. Any outbound orders due that weekend must be staged and manifested by end of shift on Thursday, May 21.

## New Handheld Scanners
Starting **April 6**, we are replacing the old Symbol scanners with the new **Zebra TC22** units. Key points:

- Each picker is assigned one device; sign it out at the cage and return it at end of shift.
- The new units use a **swappable battery** — grab a charged spare from the wall dock if you drop below 20%.
- Report any cracked screens to the shift lead immediately; do not tape them.
- Training sessions run the first week of April; a 20-minute session is required before you can badge onto the floor.

## Parking Changes
The north lot will be **repaved from April 13 to April 24**. During that window, park in the **east gravel lot** by Dock 7. Handicap spaces remain available by the main entrance. Do not park along the fire lane on the south side — vehicles will be towed at the owner's expense.
