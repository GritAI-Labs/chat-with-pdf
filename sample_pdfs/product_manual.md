# Aeris S2 Smart Thermostat — User Manual

Thank you for choosing the **Aeris S2**. This manual covers setup, features, troubleshooting, and warranty. Model number: **AER-S2-2026**.

## 1. In the Box
- Aeris S2 thermostat display unit
- Wall mounting plate and trim ring
- 4 wall screws and 2 drywall anchors
- Quick-start card
- USB-C charging cable (for the internal backup battery)

## 2. Technical Specifications
- **Display:** 3.5-inch color LCD, 320 x 480 resolution
- **Connectivity:** Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz only (does **not** support 5 GHz networks)
- **Power:** 24V C-wire required; internal rechargeable backup battery lasts up to 8 hours
- **Compatibility:** Works with most 24V forced-air, heat pump, and radiant systems. **Not compatible** with high-voltage (120V/240V) baseboard heaters.
- **Sensors:** Temperature, humidity, occupancy (PIR motion), ambient light
- **Operating range:** 40°F to 90°F setpoint

## 3. Installation
1. **Turn off power** to your HVAC system at the breaker. This is mandatory.
2. Remove your old thermostat and photograph the wiring first.
3. Connect the wires to the labeled terminals on the mounting plate. The **C (common) wire is required** for the S2 to power continuously.
4. Snap the display onto the plate and restore power at the breaker.
5. Follow the on-screen setup to join your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.

## 4. The Aeris App
Download the **Aeris Home** app (iOS 15+ or Android 10+). The app enables scheduling, remote control, energy reports, and software updates. Create one account per household; up to **6 family members** can be invited to control the same thermostat.

### Scheduling
Set up to **4 periods per day** (Wake, Away, Home, Sleep). Geofencing can automatically switch to Away mode when everyone's phone leaves a configurable radius (default 3 miles).

## 5. Energy Saving Features
- **Smart Eco** learns your patterns and can reduce heating/cooling runtime by up to 18% after two weeks.
- **Filter reminders** trigger every 90 days of runtime.
- Monthly energy reports are emailed on the 1st of each month.

## 6. Troubleshooting
- **Blank screen:** Confirm the C-wire is connected. Without it, the backup battery drains in about 8 hours and the unit shuts down.
- **Won't join Wi-Fi:** The S2 supports 2.4 GHz only. If your router broadcasts a combined 2.4/5 GHz network under one name, temporarily separate the bands or use a guest 2.4 GHz network.
- **Temperature reads high:** Keep the unit out of direct sunlight; the ambient light sensor and enclosure can bias readings near a sunny window.
- **Motion not detected:** The occupancy sensor has a 120-degree field of view and a 20-foot range. Mount at 52-60 inches above the floor.
